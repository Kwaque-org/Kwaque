# Kwaque Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Kwaque contributors, maintainers, and community members must help keep project
spaces respectful, technically constructive, and safe. Welcoming behavior
includes assuming good faith, giving actionable criticism, acknowledging
different experience levels, and focusing disagreement on the work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private
information, and personal attacks are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in Kwaque repositories, issue trackers, reviews, community
forums, events, and when a person officially represents the project.

## Reporting

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Submit a private report through
the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form and prefix its title
with `[Conduct]`. Include the relevant links, dates, behavior, and any context
that helps the maintainers investigate. If private vulnerability reporting is
temporarily unavailable, use the repository owner's private contact method and
identify the message as a Kwaque conduct report.

Maintainers will acknowledge a report, limit disclosure to people needed for
the investigation, avoid conflicts of interest, and communicate the outcome to
the reporter when possible. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself
a violation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a private warning, restrict
participation, impose a temporary ban, or permanently remove a participant.
Consequences are based on impact, pattern, intent, and the safety of the
community. A maintainer named in a report must not decide that report.
